## Tools & agents
- **GhostApproval** — Wiz Research drops a symlink attack that hits **6 major AI coding assistants**, Claude Code included, alongside Cursor, Amazon Q, Augment, Google Antigravity, Windsurf. Malicious repo plants a symlink named `project_settings.json` that actually points at `~/.ssh/authorized_keys`. README says "add this line to settings." Agent resolves it, writes it, approval dialog shows the harmless fake name — you approve blind. AWS, Cursor, Google patched fast. Two vendors went quiet. One called it "outside threat model." Published July 8. [Wiz writeup](https://www.wiz.io/blog/ghostapproval-a-trust-boundary-gap-in-ai-coding-assistants) · [coverage](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/ghostapproval-symlink-flaws-could-let.html)

## The meta
Six agents you trust with your filesystem, and one bad README can turn "set up the workspace" into "here's my SSH key." The approval dialog lied to the people who patched it late. That's the job now: you're not just writing code, you're the last sanity check between a symlink and your own machine. Stay paranoid, stay fast — both at once, that's the actual skill.
