## News cycle — the one that should worry you
**A frontier lab just shipped, on purpose, a model that answers 95% of "how do I build this exploit chain" — to *anyone it approves*, gated by a hardware key and a vetting form.** That's not a jailbreak, that's a product tier. The logic is sound (defenders need the same edge attackers will eventually get for free from a leaked or open model) but the sequencing is the story: OpenAI paused Astra last week because it couldn't rule out autonomous exploit-chaining at Critical, then this week shipped a *different* model one rung below that same threshold, on purpose, as a paid tier. The gate is a form and a YubiKey. Forms get social-engineered. Keys get phished. **Whatever access-control story you're trusting to keep a "High-cyber" model out of the wrong hands, assume it fails eventually — and assume the queries GPT-5.6-Cyber can now answer are queries every other frontier lab is one product decision away from also answering.**
