## 100x Patterns — Time Arbitrage

- **Two-agent workflows** are normal now: one agent explores/plans, a second executes, they cross-check. Redundancy as a feature, not waste.
- **Scheduled/background agents** (Cursor Automations, Cowork scheduled tasks) = work continues while you sleep. Time arbitrage stopped being theoretical.
- **Verification pipelines are the new alpha** — not raw generation speed. The devs winning right now are the ones who built trust layers around their agents, not the ones with the fastest prompt.
